Welcome to Himachal Pradesh

Himachal Pradesh is a mountainous state in northern India, often called Devbhumi or the "Land of the Gods"
because of its many ancient temples and spiritual centers. Himachal Pradesh is a beautiful state of India.
It is famous for mountains, valleys, rivers and beautiful places.

Key Facts & Geography

• Capitals: Shimla (summer) and Dharamshala (winter).
• Area & Districts: Spans 55,673 square kilometers across 12 districts.
• Rivers: Drained by five major perennial rivers: the Beas, Chenab, Ravi, Sutlej, and Yamuna.
• Terrain: Ranging from the lower Shivalik Hills to the high-altitude Great Himalayas, with peaks like Reo Purgyil
reaching 6,820 meters

About Himachal Pradesh

Himachal Pradesh is one of the most beautiful states of India. It is famous for its snow-covered mountains,
green valleys, beautiful rivers, forests and peaceful surroundings. The state is a popular destination for tourists,
nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts.

The name Himachal means "Land of Snow". The state is surrounded by the Himalayas and has many beautiful
hill stations. Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala, Dalhousie and Kullu are some of its famous tourist destinations.

Famous Tourist Places

  • Shimla: Famous for its beautiful hills, Mall Road and colonial architecture.
  • Manali: Known for mountains, valleys, rivers and adventure activities.
  • Dharamshala: Famous for Tibetan culture and beautiful mountain views.
  • Dalhousie: A peaceful hill station surrounded by green mountains.
  • Kullu: Famous for its beautiful valley, temples and traditional festivals.

Culture & Festivals

Himachal Pradesh has a rich culture and traditional lifestyle. People celebrate many colourful festivals
with traditional music, dance and local customs. Kullu Dussehra is one of the most famous festivals of the state.

Traditional Himachali clothes, handicrafts, folk dances and local food are an important part of the state's culture.

Adventure Activities

  • 🏔️ Trekking in the Himalayas
  • 🎿 Skiing in snow-covered areas
  • 🪂 Paragliding
  • 🚣 River rafting
  • 🏕️ Camping

Climate

Himachal Pradesh experiences different types of climate because of its varied altitude.
Summers are generally pleasant in the hill stations, while winters can be very cold with snowfall
in many higher areas. The monsoon season brings rainfall and makes the valleys green and beautiful.

Why Visit Himachal Pradesh?

Himachal Pradesh is a perfect destination for people who love nature, mountains and peaceful places.
Visitors can enjoy beautiful landscapes, explore ancient temples, experience local culture and participate in
exciting adventure activities.

From peaceful valleys to snow-covered mountains, Himachal Pradesh offers something for every traveller.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Himachal Pradesh depends on the place and activities you want to enjoy.
Spring and summer are suitable for exploring hill stations and valleys, while winter is popular for snowfall
and winter activities in higher regions.
