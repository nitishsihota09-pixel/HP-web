About Himachal Pradesh

Himachal Pradesh is situated in the northern part of India. It is known for its beautiful mountains, green valleys and forests.

The capital of Himachal Pradesh is Shimla. The state became a full state on 25 January 1971.

Some Information

  • Capital: Shimla
  • Location: Northern India
  • Statehood: 25 January 1971
  • Famous for: Mountains and Tourism
  • Climate: Cold and pleasant
